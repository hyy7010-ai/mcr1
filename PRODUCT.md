# GraceFlow — Product Context

## Register
**Product** (app UI / dashboard). Design serves the task: managing a church's people, services, and resources. Not a marketing site.

## What it is
A multi-tenant, multilingual church management web app (CMS/CRM for churches). One place for member care, service scheduling (rosters), small groups, publications, prayer, giving, and internal communication. Runs in the browser; no install.

## Target users
- **Managers** (pastors, ministry leads): full administration — approvals, rostering, groups, finances.
- **Staff** (worship/AV/hospitality teams): their roster, availability/leave, resources.
- **Group leaders**: their group's info, posts, members (can also hold a Manager/Staff role).
- **Members** (congregation): public info, their group, prayer wall, publications.
- **Super admin**: platform-level, manages all churches.

Many congregations are multilingual/immigrant, so 6 languages ship (zh-CN, zh-TW, en, ja, ko, th).

## Purpose / desired outcome
Reduce admin load for ministry leaders and raise congregation participation. The roster, in particular, must make "who serves when" obvious at a glance and prevent scheduling people who marked themselves unavailable.

## Brand personality
Warm, calm, trustworthy, quietly editorial — a church office that feels cared-for, not a corporate SaaS dashboard. Serif display type (Playfair) over a clean sans (Inter) gives it a printed-bulletin warmth. Restrained, not loud.

## Anti-references (what to avoid)
- Generic navy/indigo SaaS dashboards, purple→blue gradients, gradient text.
- Cold, dense enterprise admin panels.
- Nested cards, side-stripe accent borders, tiny all-caps eyebrows on every block.
- Childish/clip-art "church" clichés (doves, stained glass as decoration).

## Strategic design principles
1. **Glanceable rosters.** A leader should read a month and know coverage, gaps, holidays, birthdays, and conflicts without clicking.
2. **Role-aware surfaces.** Same app, different depth per role; never show a member the manager's controls.
3. **Calm hierarchy.** One ink color does most of the work; color is reserved for meaning (roles, conflict/unavailable = red, holidays).
4. **Bilingual-safe layout.** Every label must survive Chinese and English without wrapping or truncating.
5. **Identity preservation.** The warm-paper palette + Playfair/Inter is the established brand; refine within it, don't replace it.
